# PRODUCT NAME: MediQ

# PRODUCT OVERVIEW

An application that helps paitients to easiely book appointments and meet up with up with consultations, as 
well as live queuing, updates and tracking their activities(lab, pharmacy etc).


# PROBLEM STATEMENT

Paitents waiting in long lines and queues in an overcrowded room not sure when they will meet the doctor as well 
as staffs, feeling overwhelmed in managing many patients manually leading to disoraganization. 
Through some research and based on my personal experience when i was a paitient, some hospitals especially mine
tried to use paper method to keep track of the patients coming in for check ups or appointments but, still its an 
ineffective method.
   Why is it ineffective? The paitents must be physically present in the hospital to have a queuing number and the 
patient will still be unaware of when they will see the doctor as they will not know were they stand in their 
chances to see the doctor especially in a crowded hospital. Impatient patient may result in cheating the syetem 
by pushing through lines or may lie as well causing everywhere to be rowdy. Not only the paitents but the staff 
as well, it will be overwhelming for receptionist to  keep track especially for large numbers, mistakes  can 
occur. 
  Futher interviews i conducted shows that most patients who visits the hospital and experiences such problem; leave 
the building with frustration, very late compared to their time of arrival or don't even see the doctor at the end 
of the day despite their efforts, discouraging those coming from far. 
  A patient even stated that even given an appointed time, its useless because he will end up waiting because there's no 
clear order and when emergency cases come through it scatters the whole thing with no updates from the nurses.


# PROPOSED SOLUTION

To create an application that paitients and staff can easily access. The app enbles users to register for medical 
appointments by choosing a convenient date and time. It also helps administrative staff to keep track of the amount 
of patients coming in and to avoid doctors being double-booked.
They will be assigned dates, digital queuing numbers, live updates and a tracking system to track their
activites; which will constantly be updating. it also enables to alert users when the doctor is ready to see you. 


# BUSINESS OBJECTIVES

- To build paitents trust.
- Making appointments easy and faster for their paitients
- Increase the hospital credibility
- Increase public awareness of the hospital and attract new patients.



# TARGET USERS

- The paitents
- The front desk receptionist
- The medical staffs and personel


# USER STORY

- As a patient, i want fast and easy appointments as well as queuing updates so that i know when my turn is approaching.
- As a receptionist, I want to view the daily appointment schedule so that i can organise patient arrivals. 
- As a receptionist, i want to manage appointment slots so that patients can only book at available times. 
- As a paitient, i want to able to see my updates live as well as track my activities in the hospital like my lab and pharmacy visits.


# FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

FR-01-The system should allow patients to create accounts
FR-02-Patients should be able to book available appointments
FR-03-The system should assign queue numbers to registered patients.
FR-04-Patients should be able to view their current queue status as well being given updates if anything changes. 
FR-05-Patients should be able to change their appointment dates or times.


# NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIRMENTS

- Common patient and staff pages should load within 3 seconds under the agreed load
- Ensuring passwords are never stored in plaintext passwords.
- The application should be able to run on common mobile browsers and desktop browsers.
- The system should be able to accomodate more departments in the hosiptal as the hospital expands
- The system should show a clear error when the service is unavailable.
- Patients must not access another patient's booking or queue information.



# MVP FEATURES

- Patients and staff registration or logins
- Appointment booking
- Queue number generation
- live Status updates
- Tracking of paitients activites
- Hospital staffs interface/dashboard


# SUCCESS METRICS

- Successful registered paitents and staff
- Easing going appointments scheduling
- Reduced numbers of awaiting paitents
- Queuing tracking is accurate
- constant updating of live tracking.
